Privacy Policy

This policy explains what ElevateHub and ElevateFlow collect, what stays on your computer, and what is shared with services you choose to connect.

1. Scope and who we are

ElevateHub is an independent live-production software suite created and operated by Jeremy Jarega in Australia. This policy applies to the ElevateHub desktop app, ElevateFlow, associated tools and plugins, and the ElevateHub website.

It does not replace the privacy policies of third-party services that you connect or visit, such as Google, Planning Center, GitHub, YouTube, Resolume, ProPresenter, OBS, or websites opened in the Production Browser.

2. Information we collect

Account and profile information

When you create or use an account, we may store your email address, display name, nickname, profile image, Firebase user identifier, team memberships, role, and account creation information. Authentication credentials are processed by Firebase Authentication.

Teams and operator PINs

Team administrators can add operator names and roles. Four-digit operator PINs are stored as salted cryptographic hashes, not as readable PINs. Team administrators are responsible for having permission to add their team members' information.

Cloud sync and configuration

Cloud Firestore may hold account profiles, teams, team membership and roles, access settings, update configuration, and temporary presentation sync sessions such as a session code, slide position, and update time.

Local app data

Most production content and preferences are kept on the device, including presentation libraries, slides, media references, Resolume Control sets and button designs, operator workspace layouts, service times, endpoint addresses, and other app settings. Depending on the feature, this information may be stored in local app storage or files you select.

Usage and technical information

The desktop app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. Its default implementation may collect app-instance identifiers, session and usage statistics, approximate location derived from network information, and device, operating-system, and app-version information. ElevateHub does not intentionally send names, email addresses, slide content, media, PINs, or connected-service credentials as analytics events.

Website information

The website is hosted by Netlify. Netlify and the website's content delivery providers may receive technical request information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and timestamps for delivery, security, and operational logging. The website also requests release information and downloads from the public ElevateHub downloads repository on GitHub.

3. How we use information

  • To create accounts, sign users in, and show the correct team profile.
  • To keep each operator's settings and layouts separate.
  • To provide cloud-assisted presentation sync and restricted feature access.
  • To check for, download, and install app updates.
  • To operate, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the software.
  • To respond to support, privacy, and account requests.

ElevateHub does not sell personal information and does not use account information for targeted advertising.

4. When information is shared

We disclose information only as needed to operate the suite, at your direction, or where required by law.

  • Google Firebase: authentication, Firestore database services, and app analytics.
  • Netlify and content delivery providers: website hosting and delivery.
  • GitHub: public release metadata, application updates, downloads, and support requests.
  • Services you connect: the app communicates with services and devices you configure, including Resolume, ProPresenter, OBS, Planning Center, NDI endpoints, YouTube, and user-selected websites. Those communications may occur over your local network or directly with the third party.
  • Legal and safety: where reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users, investigate abuse, or protect the security of ElevateHub.

Google, GitHub, Netlify, and other providers may process information outside Australia, including in the United States and other locations where they or their subprocessors operate.

5. Storage and retention

Cloud account and team information is generally retained while the account or team remains active and for a reasonable period afterward where needed for security, support, legal obligations, or recovery. Temporary sync records are intended for active sync sessions, but may remain until deleted by the app or cleaned up administratively. Service providers may retain backups and logs under their own schedules.

Local data remains on the device until you remove it through the app, delete the relevant files or app data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling may not remove documents or media saved outside the app's data folder.

6. Access, correction, and deletion

You can edit some profile and team information inside the app. To ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information or an ElevateHub account, submit a request through the Support page. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Some information may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or resolving disputes.

You can remove locally stored information from the app or device. You control whether to connect third-party services and may disconnect them or remove locally saved endpoint details at any time.

If you believe we have mishandled your personal information, contact us through Support with the subject “Privacy request.” We will review the complaint and respond within a reasonable time. Australian users may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

7. Security and children

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including provider-managed authentication and salted hashing for team PINs. No system or network transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Team PINs are convenience controls for shared production environments and are not a substitute for a strong account password or device security.

ElevateHub is designed for production teams. A child should not create an account or provide personal information without the approval and supervision of a parent, guardian, school, church, or other responsible organisation.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the suite, providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated, and material changes may also be announced in the app or release notes.

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, and complaints, use the ElevateHub Support page. For information about Google's handling of Firebase and Analytics data, see Firebase Privacy and Security and Google's Privacy Policy.
