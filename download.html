INSTALLATION

From download to show-ready.

ElevateHub handles the studios after installation. Because current builds are independently distributed, your operating system may show a security prompt.

On macOS

  1. 1Open the downloaded DMG and move ElevateHub to Applications.
  2. 2If macOS blocks it, open Terminal and run:
xattr -rd com.apple.quarantine /Applications/ElevateHub.app
  1. 3Open ElevateHub from Applications.

On Windows

  1. 1Run the downloaded ElevateHub installer.
  2. 2If SmartScreen appears, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. 3Launch ElevateHub from the Start menu.
